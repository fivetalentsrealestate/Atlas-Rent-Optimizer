How this works

three things set your rent

Where to find your numbers

Parcel counts by rarity and your Atlas Bucks balance are on your profile screen. Badge count is in your passport. Nothing here connects to your account — you type the figures in.

Rarity sets your base

Every parcel earns a fixed trickle per second by rarity. That base is the one thing boosts and badges multiply, so a big portfolio of commons still starts small.

Badges are a step function

Multipliers land at 1, 11, 31, 61 and 101 badges — +5% each. Badges between thresholds pay nothing, so buying one at a time only helps if it completes a step.

The tier trap

Ad-boost multipliers drop in bands as your parcel count grows — 30× under 150 parcels down to 2× past 1,500. Crossing a boundary can cut your income, so a well-timed stop beats buying whenever you can.

SRB ignores your tier

During a Super Rent Boost every player gets 50× on all parcels up to 150,000, whatever band they're in. For heavy players these weekends can be half the year's rent — and they soften the tier trap considerably.

What this can't tell you

Nothing about whether the game is worth your time or money. It converts your inputs into rent, so you can compare choices — the entertainment value is yours to weigh.
